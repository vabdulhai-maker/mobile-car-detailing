A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

How does mobile detailing work?

In this concept, you select a package and request a visit at your home or workplace. A real operator would confirm access, utilities, parking requirements, and timing before the appointment.

How long should I set aside?

Allow roughly 1–2 hours for exterior care, 3–4 hours for a complete detail, and a longer appointment for ceramic work. These are demo estimates; a real quote would confirm timing and coating cure requirements.

What if it rains?

A real operator would check weather and available covered space before confirming a visit. If conditions are unsuitable, the appointment would need to be rescheduled.

Can I book an appointment on this site?

You can try the full seven-step booking flow. This is a demonstration: requests are saved on your current device only. Nothing is sent to a business, no appointment is confirmed, and no payment is collected.

Are the prices and photos real?

Prices and service areas are illustrative. Images are AI-created, and the finish comparison is simulated. No customer work, ratings, or verified results are claimed.
